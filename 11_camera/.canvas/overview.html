Module 11 Overview: Camera

CSC-244 Android Development With Kotlin

Overview

In this module, you will learn how to take photos with the device camera and save them locally. Work on Android apps in Android Studio installed on your computer. Grader Than Workspace supports demonstrations, basic Kotlin examples, and server components. Complete one module each week. Submit assigned app demonstration videos in Canvas; you may revise and resubmit until the end of class. Start early and leave time to test.

Key Concepts

  • Camera capture: taking a new photo through a device camera
  • Photo result: the image returned after a capture action
  • Local photo storage: keeping a captured image on the device
  • CameraX: an Android library option for camera features

Outcomes

Upon completion of this module, students will be able to:

  • Start a simple photo-taking task.
  • Describe what the app should do after a photo is taken.
  • Save a captured photo locally.
  • Identify CameraX as an optional camera development resource.

Guiding Questions

Consider the following questions as you review the learning resources this week:

  • Why might a user need to capture a photo inside an app?
  • What should happen when a user finishes or cancels a photo?
  • How can an app make it clear that a photo was saved?
