Module 10 Overview: Notifications

CSC-244 Android Development With Kotlin

Overview

In this module, we will learn how to create and display notifications to the user. Work on Android apps in Android Studio installed on your computer. Grader Than Workspace supports demonstrations, basic Kotlin examples, and server components. Complete one module each week. Submit assigned app demonstration videos in Canvas; you may revise and resubmit until the end of class. Start early and leave time to test.

Key Concepts

  • Notification: a message Android can show outside the app screen
  • Notification channel: a category of notifications with shared user controls
  • Notification group: related notifications shown together
  • Notification action: a user choice that opens relevant app content

Outcomes

Upon completion of this module, students will be able to:

  • Create and display a simple notification.
  • Describe notifications shown while the app is not in focus.
  • Organize notifications using channels and groups.
  • Open an activity from a notification.

Guiding Questions

Consider the following questions as you review the learning resources this week:

  • What information deserves to interrupt a user?
  • How can grouping notifications reduce clutter?
  • Where should a notification take someone who taps it?
