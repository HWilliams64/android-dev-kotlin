Module 15 Overview: Work on the Final Project

CSC-244 Android Development With Kotlin

Overview

In this module, you will spend your time working on the Final Project. You will learn about user interface prototyping. You will gain an understanding of how to perform market research on your app before development and assess the viability of your concept. Work on Android apps in Android Studio installed on your computer. Grader Than Workspace supports demonstrations, basic Kotlin examples, and server components. Complete one module each week. Submit assigned app demonstration videos in Canvas; you may revise and resubmit until the end of class. Start early and leave time to test.

Key Concepts

  • User need: a problem the final app aims to solve
  • UI prototype: an early model used to explore screens and interactions
  • Market research: learning about users and existing solutions
  • App viability: whether an idea can work within real needs and limits

Outcomes

Upon completion of this module, students will be able to:

  • Identify the user need for your chosen final app.
  • Use a prototype to explain the main user journey.
  • Compare your idea with existing solutions through market research.
  • Build and demonstrate a feasible version of your chosen application.

Guiding Questions

Consider the following questions as you review the learning resources this week:

  • What user problem does your app solve?
  • What can existing apps teach you about your idea’s value?
  • Which features fit the time and resources you have?
