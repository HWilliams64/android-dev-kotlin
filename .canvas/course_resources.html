Course Resources

CSC-244 Android Development With Kotlin

Textbook

There is no required textbook. Find all learning materials through Canvas Modules.

  • Module 1 — Kotlin Basics: Jupyter notebooks combine reading, demonstrations, runnable Kotlin examples, and practice.
  • Modules 2–15: Use each module’s Resources page for the videos to watch and the required and optional readings listed in the course plan.
  • Learning order: Open Canvas Modules and complete the Learning Materials before the Deliverables. Review the slides, watch the videos, read the assigned resources, and practice. Then complete the graded discussion and any assignment or project work listed for that week.

Writing and Running Code

You must install Android Studio on your computer. Write and test your Android apps there. Read the Android Studio installation instructions.

  • Grader Than Workspace: This browser-based development environment inside Canvas supports demonstrations, basic Kotlin examples, and server components.
  • App development: Use Android Studio for general app programming, interface design, and app testing.
  • Opening the workspace: Locate Grader Than in the Canvas left-side menu, select it, then wait for Workspace to connect.

Assignments

Assignments are mini-projects in which you create Android apps. The apps increase in complexity as you learn.

  • Submission: Upload a video of yourself using your app to the assignment in Canvas. Show the app’s required behavior clearly.
  • Feedback: Check the Canvas assignment for instructor feedback. App assignments are not submitted to or graded by Grader Than.
  • Resubmissions: You may revise and resubmit as many times as you would like until the end of class. Follow the assignment’s posted due date for your initial submission.
  • Weekly discussions: Complete the graded discussion in each module. Connect the module’s concepts to a real-world app, focusing on user needs and observable behavior.
  • Plan ahead: Start early and allow time for design, implementation, testing, debugging, and recording.

Course Projects

Note-Taking App — Module 12: Connect your app to a REST API, a web service that lets the app read and send information. Final Project — Module 15: Create an application of your choice. Prepare your proposal and user interface prototype in Module 13.

Use Android Studio for both projects. Record a video demonstrating your app and upload it to Canvas. You are free to choose your final app’s purpose.
