Module 13 Overview: Deployment

CSC-244 Android Development With Kotlin

Overview

In this module, you will learn how to publish an app on Google Play and monitor its health through crash reports. You will also prepare your final-project proposal and prototype. Work on Android apps in Android Studio installed on your computer. Grader Than Workspace supports demonstrations, basic Kotlin examples, and server components. Complete one module each week. Submit assigned app demonstration videos in Canvas; you may revise and resubmit until the end of class. Start early and leave time to test.

Key Concepts

  • App publishing: preparing an app for distribution through Google Play
  • Deployment automation: tools that reduce repeated release steps
  • Crash reporting: collecting information about app failures after release
  • Prototype: an early model of an app’s screens and interactions

Outcomes

Upon completion of this module, students will be able to:

  • Describe the main steps in publishing an Android app.
  • Explain how Fastlane can support repeated deployment work.
  • Compare the purpose of the listed crash-reporting tools.
  • Prepare the final-project proposal and user interface prototype.

Guiding Questions

Consider the following questions as you review the learning resources this week:

  • What must a team consider before users receive an app release?
  • How can reports from deployed apps guide improvements?
  • What can a prototype reveal before a full app is built?
